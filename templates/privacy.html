Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic information such as pages visited and actions taken, used to improve the service.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Display your expense history and summaries within the app.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Diagnose bugs and improve app performance.
  • Send essential service communications (e.g., password resets).

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers located in secured data centres. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics) to operate the service. These providers process data only on our behalf and are contractually bound to keep it confidential.

We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks, social media trackers, or data brokers. Any future integrations will be disclosed in an update to this policy.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses only essential session cookies required to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying signed in.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or wish to make a data request, please reach out through the contact information provided within the app.
